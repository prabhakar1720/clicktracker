Reading the numbers

Difference = Affise raw clicks − AppsFlyer clicks. Gap % = difference / Affise × 100. Negative values mean AppsFlyer is higher. A zero Affise denominator shows N/A. A gap above 10% in either direction is marked for review. API errors retain prior values as stale and exclude them from paired totals. Numeric AF click rows are summed even when other rows are blank/N/A. Such subtotals are clearly marked Partial; incomplete totals are excluded from gap calculations. Only an explicit matching AF row with zero clicks is shown as zero. Empty reports, missing dates and unmatched PID/agency rows show unavailable data with response diagnostics. Earlier unverified zeros require a fresh fetch.

AppsFlyer agency visibility is limited to the apps and traffic the account can access. Daily boundaries, timezones, redirect delivery, validation and reporting delays can explain differences; a gap alone does not prove a delivery failure.
